From the founders

From a dyslexic founder, to your little one.

We're not a big corporate, and we're not tied to any tech giant. We're just a bunch of college students who realised we'd become addicted to social media - and that it isn't good for us.

When we were kids, we wanted to be astronauts and scientists. Today, more than half of kids want to be influencers. We don't think that's good - every child should get the chance to be a dreamer and a wanderer.

The early years shape a whole life. So we designed NijO - the good influence: the first product in the world architecturally designed to minimise its own use and maximise real-world connection.

From day one we've been transparent about what we're building, and how - documenting all of it on Instagram. Because between you and us, the most important thing is trust. Your kids are the ones who'll use NijO.

So, we present NijO - the companion for your kids, with its own personality, mood swings, flaws, and a terrible sense of humour.
