6. AI conversations - what Nijo is, and what it is not

6.1 AI output is generated, and it can be wrong

Nijo's replies come from large language models. They are generated, not read from a vetted script. This means:

  • Nijo can be factually wrong, and can be confidently wrong.
  • Despite our safety prompts and filters, Nijo may occasionally say something inappropriate, upsetting, or biased - something you would not want a child to hear. We work hard to prevent this. We cannot promise it will never happen.
  • Nijo's replies are not educational, medical, psychological, mental-health, or any other professional advice, for you or your child. Do not rely on them as such.

Nijo does not replace parental attention. If your child is distressed or in danger, a person must handle that - not a toy.

6.2 The alerts feature, and its limits

Nijo can automatically scan conversations for concerning topics and send you a notification with a short snippet of what was said.

This is a best-effort convenience feature, not a safeguarding system. It:

  • will miss things - it is an automated classifier, and not an exhaustive one;
  • will sometimes flag harmless things;
  • depends on notifications actually reaching your phone, which depends on your phone, your network, and your notification settings - all outside our control;
  • is not monitored by a human at our end. Nobody at Neuro Sapiens watches your child's conversations in real time, and nobody will intervene.

Do not rely on Nijo alerts to know whether your child is safe. If you are worried about your child's wellbeing, contact the appropriate professional or emergency service. To the fullest extent the law allows, we are not liable for harm arising from an alert that was not generated, not delivered, delivered late, or misread.
